**Status:** The first two research chapters and the derived full-restoration ending for the current 174-item laboratory are implemented and focused-verified. A focused E2E now confirms the completed chronology state survives browser reload. The cumulative agent-led fresh-origin run completed both chapter loops and puzzles; its manual post-chronology reload and independent human feedback remain outstanding. ## Included

A fresh game opens in one continuous navigable laboratory with 174 deterministic entities: the original 150 canonical items and 24 authored chapter-two records. The new records form three eight-item collections: **The Bell Before Dawn**, **Meridian Survey**, and **Parallax Trials**. Each collection mixes two handwritten notes, two experiment records, two map fragments, and two formula cards, and has its own contained eight-slot destination.

The slice also includes the Harmonic Calibration folio, four general filing destinations, two scratch pads, the item inspector, notebook and progress panels, save dialog, and three visible Palimpsest Engine states: a Dormant brass frame with dim, empty lens; a faceted illuminated Memory Crystal; and a Chronology Wheel that keeps the crystal and adds a toothed, spoked wheel. Both 540 × 530 scratch pads start with 24 spaces. The passive one-point **Modular Worktables** skill adds a sixth row to each existing board for 30 total spaces, without adding a pad or changing its footprint.

## Player interactions and progression

The multi-selection Inspector shows every selected item's title, family, and clue as a semantic definition list. Its details scroll independently while the group Move controls remain pinned, including when all 40 available items are selected.

1. Pan and zoom through the world, including keyboard navigation, and return to items or destinations through world controls. Touch supports two-finger pinch zoom anchored at the moving midpoint; when one pinch finger lifts, the remaining finger continues as a one-finger pan. A second touch during item drag cancels the drag without dropping or moving the item. Camera changes use the existing save path and add no save fields. The Palimpsest Engine jump centers the Engine at 100% when it fits and scales down to keep it fully visible on narrow screens. The same jump selector lists unfinished collections with live correctly-filed counts and focuses the first loose or misfiled member; it derives status from actual placement and removes a collection when all members are filed correctly.
2. Drag objects into scratch pads to compare them. Workspace and scratch-pad placement is not validated as final filing.
3. Select an object to inspect its category and clues. Keyboard activation moves focus to the inspector's target selector.
4. File an object with a pointer drop or the inspector Move control. Both use the same capacity-aware slot allocation. A new arrival to a full target is rejected with feedback; moving an existing occupant within that full target is allowed.
5. A correct final filing is confirmed. A wrong filing stays in the chosen destination, is clearly marked, and can be moved again.
6. Cancel an active drag or pan with pointercancel or Escape. The item or camera returns to its pre-gesture state.
7. Complete Harmonic Calibration to gain one skill point and unlock the pulse-sequence puzzle. The Resonance Lens highlights related items but never files them.
8. Complete the three chapter-two collections. Each collection grants one skill point once and records its notebook clue. After the last collection, unlock **Chronology of the Last Bell**.
9. Purchase and use the **Missing Member Compass** on a selected chapter collection. It highlights a loose member that needs filing and never moves, files, or validates an item.
10. Purchase **Modular Worktables** for one insight point. Its passive upgrade expands each pad from 24 to 30 spaces; arrangements remain temporary and unvalidated until each item is filed.
11. Order the chapter events from the dated evidence and solve the chronology puzzle. The notebook records **The Last Bell**, and the Palimpsest Engine advances to **Chronology Wheel**.
12. Autosave and export compressed saves. V1 and v2 saves migrate to v3; valid legacy autosaves are preserved in recovery. The new ownership uses the existing v3 `progress.ownedSkills` field, so the schema stays v3. V3 accepts slots 24–29 only when the skill is owned; legacy migration keeps its existing skill whitelist. Strict validation rejects malformed state before an import can change the live game or stored backup.
13. Purchase the optional two-point **Aether Wayfinder** and activate it for a selected item that is not correctly filed, whether it is in the workspace, scratch pad, or misfiled in a destination. The camera focuses the canonical destination at a minimum preferred scale of 0.72, and a visible, accessible **For this item** guide lasts seven seconds. The skill never moves or validates the item; one Wayfinder live status names the item and destination. Reduced-motion settings suppress the highlight animation while retaining its static cue. Ownership uses the existing v3 `progress.ownedSkills` field and leaves the schema unchanged.
14. Open the content-free **Clockwork Atlas** minimap to inspect item-density bins, destination/scratch-pad/Engine landmarks, and the clipped camera outline. Click or drag to recenter without changing zoom, or use arrow keys to move a crosshair and Enter to recenter. Escape closes Atlas and returns focus to its toggle. Atlas and Item Finder cannot be open together. On 390 × 480 and 320 × 480 the map starts collapsed; reduced-motion settings make map and camera response immediate. The map adds no entities, content, or saved fields; camera recentering uses the existing saved camera.

The Save & Restore dialog also provides three fixed browser-local manual save slots. New records wrap compressed v3 saves in a version 1 envelope with slot ID and saved time, without adding save-schema fields. A raw legacy save under `MANUAL_SAVE_KEY` remains available as Slot 1 with unknown date until the player confirms an overwrite. Empty-slot saves are immediate; overwriting and deleting require confirmation. Restoring requires confirmation, uses the existing import validation, and backs up the current game under `BACKUP_SAVE_KEY` before applying the save. Corrupt slots and storage failures are reported without damaging the live game or other slots.

Accessible multi-selection is available through a toolbar toggle. Pointer, touch, and keyboard activation toggles membership without dragging; the inspected item carries into the group, each item exposes `aria-pressed`, and the selected count is visible. Selection is transient, excluded from the save schema, and capped at 40 objects. The Canvas overview keeps its 64-total DOM overlay cap, including selected and other forced cards. The Inspector can move the group to a destination using staged allocation that treats selected occupants as vacated and commits only when every item fits. Capacity failure leaves items, progress, rewards, events, and saves unchanged and retains selection. Successful movement validates every item independently, preserving incorrect filings as visibly misfiled, then updates collection/puzzle progression and resulting rewards/events once from the final state. The single-item movement flow is unchanged.

The world viewport uses a uniform-grid spatial index to cull offscreen DOM items. The Engine's culling AABB uses a top-left `position` computed as center minus half its dimensions, while its visual `.invention-anchor` stays centered; partial viewport overlap therefore keeps its clipped edge mounted. Decorative stage markup is hidden from assistive technology, visible stage labels and descriptions remain available, and the wheel respects saved and OS reduced-motion preferences. The world-ground layer adds static, low-contrast survey rings and panel seams that follow camera pan and zoom beneath Canvas and cards; it remains hidden from assistive technology and non-interactive. These visuals do not change progression, the 174-entity count, or save data. Filed items fit their canonical slot bounds while the workspace keeps its existing 78px visual minimum. Fresh filler remains seeded within the original family clusters; the first-session refinement reduced the 174-item layout from 259 overlapping axis-aligned pairs to 1 and removed full occlusions without changing authored positions or item content. Long Inspector details now scroll separately from controls so **Move item** remains visible at 1084 × 827 and 390 × 480.

At widths up to 720px, the world toolbar wraps controls into content-sized rows, preserves a minimum 154px location selector, and stays within the viewport. The mobile world hint and selection toast reposition to avoid overlapping each other or the toolbar at 320 × 480 and 390 × 480. The desktop toolbar keeps its single-row layout. These CSS changes preserve control behavior and do not alter camera or save state.

## Full-restoration ending

Completion is derived from the frozen current 174-item catalog: every expected item must be correctly filed at its canonical destination, every collection must be complete from actual member placement, and every catalogued puzzle must be solved. Missing expected IDs block completion and remain counted as unrestored. The ending presents an accessible Palimpsest Engine replay, congratulations, derived item/collection/puzzle/notebook/skill statistics, and **Continue exploring**. Completion is not stored as a new save field. The narrative identifies the Engine as a witness/recorder and presents the Professor and Elian as witnesses. The Professor chose to keep the Engine recording through an unstable echo so the account of what he and Elian witnessed would survive; his unsent apology supports that choice. The records do not establish the Event's original trigger or Elian's response.
